Two recipes, one shared helper: `njust nhm <action>` runs `nh home`, `njust nos <action>`
runs `nh os`. A bare leading token is nh's subcommand (`switch` by default); a bare token
that is not one of that scope's actions is the target, like every other recipe's bare
argument; anything else reaches nh verbatim. Actions are nh's own: home takes
switch/build, os takes switch/boot/test/build.

Under ADR-40dd18b6358e: the flake root is the installable, the target is nh's own -c/-H,
--dry-run becomes nh's --dry, and --sudo is dropped because nh elevates through its own
-e/--elevation-strategy. The remote-home guard `_deploy` already has is mirrored here,
because nh has no notion of which host the caller is on.
