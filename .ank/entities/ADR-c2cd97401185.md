constraint: |
  terraform.sh reproduces the recipe it is given: on a drive the host owns (owns=true, which must equal flakeHost.ownsWholeDisk) it writes a fresh GPT and creates every partition the recipe names; on a drive it does not own (owns=false) it may append a new entry into free space that no existing entry claims, and it never modifies, resizes, renames or retypes an entry that already exists. Every creation and every mkfs goes through the confirm gate (--force is the only non-interactive override).
